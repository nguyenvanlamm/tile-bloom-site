Appendix A

Privacy Policy

Tile Bloom is a puzzle game made to be played offline. It does not ask who you are, and it does not collect, send or sell any data about you.

Last updated 12 October 2026

§ 1What stays on your device

  • Your progress: cleared levels, stars, best moves and times, daily results and your streak.
  • Unfinished floors, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your settings: theme, haptics, assist mode.
  • Whether you have bought the full game.

All of this is stored on your device only (in the browser’s local storage on the web). It never leaves the device. Deleting the app, or clearing the site’s data in the browser, removes it. Settings → Reset progress removes your progress but keeps your purchase.

§ 2What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

This website loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers either.

§ 3Purchases

Chapter I and the daily tile are free. Buying the full game is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store that the purchase exists.

§ 4Sharing a result

When you share a daily result, the game hands a short text (puzzle number, moves, time) to your device’s share sheet or clipboard. Where it goes next is up to you.

§ 5Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

§ 6Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
