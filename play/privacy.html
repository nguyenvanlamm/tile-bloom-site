Privacy Policy

Quarterbloom · last updated 13 October 2026

Quarterbloom is a puzzle game made to be played offline. It does not ask who you are, and it does not collect, send or sell any data about you.

What is saved on your device

  • Your progress: cleared levels, stars, best moves and times, daily results and your streak.
  • Unfinished boards, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your settings: theme, haptics, assist mode.
  • Whether you have bought the full game.

All of this is saved on your device and is never sent to us. Whether it is backed up depends on where you play:

  • Android: the game opts out of Android backup and device-to-device transfer, so the data stays on the device. Deleting the app removes it.
  • iPhone and iPad: if you back up the device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS includes this data in the backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Restoring that backup brings it back. Deleting the app removes it from the device, but not from backups made before.
  • Web: the data is kept in the browser's local storage. Clearing the site's data removes it.

Settings → Reset progress removes your progress but keeps your purchase.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Chapter I and the daily tile are free. Buying the full game is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store that the purchase exists.

Sharing a result

When you share a daily result, the game hands a short text (puzzle number, moves, time) to your device's share sheet or clipboard. Where it goes next is up to you.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
