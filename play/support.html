How to play

Tap a tile to turn it clockwise; long-press (or right-click) turns it back. Every corner holds a quarter of a flower. When the corners meeting at a point match, a flower blooms there. Make every meeting point bloom to finish the floor. Fewer moves earn more stars; each hint lowers the best you can get by one star.

Purchases

Chapter I and the daily tile are free. One purchase opens every chapter after the first. Bought it before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Refunds

The full game is paid for through the App Store or Google Play, and refunds start with the store that took the payment: we never see the payment.

On iPhone and iPad, only Apple can refund a purchase. Ask from Apple's Report a Problem page, signed in with the Apple Account you paid with.

On Android, within 48 hours of buying, ask Google Play: open Budget & order history, pick the order and choose Report a problem. After 48 hours, Google asks you to contact the developer: write to us at lamnv260398@gmail.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt.

Not sure where you paid? Write to us and we will help.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device and is not synced between devices. On Android it is not backed up, so deleting the app removes it. On iPhone and iPad it is included when you back up the device with iCloud or to a computer, and restoring that backup brings it back. Settings → Reset progress starts over without removing your purchase.

The browser version

Quarterbloom also plays in a web browser, on a computer or a phone. Chapter I and the daily tile are free there too, but the full game cannot be bought in a browser: every chapter after the first is in the Android and iOS apps, and a purchase made in an app does not open them in the browser. Progress in the browser is kept in that browser's storage, apart from the apps; clearing the site's data removes it. On a computer, right-click turns a tile back, and the keyboard plays too: the arrows pick a tile, Space or Enter turns it (with Shift, back), Z undoes and H takes a hint.
