## How to play

Tap a tile to turn it 90° clockwise; long-press (or right-click) turns it back. Every corner holds a quarter of a flower. When all the corners meeting at a point match, a flower blooms there. Make every meeting point bloom to finish the floor. Fewer moves earn more stars: three stars at or under par. Each hint turns one tile right and pins it, and lowers the best you can earn by one star.

## Purchases and restoring

Chapter I and the daily tile are free. One purchase opens every chapter after the first, for good. No subscription. Bought it before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

## Refunds

Apple and Google handle all in-app purchases. For the App Store, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com); for Google Play, use your order history in the Play Store.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app (or clear the site's data in the browser). Settings → Reset progress starts over without removing your purchase.

## Playing in the browser

The web version at [nguyenvanlamm.github.io/tile-bloom-site/play/](https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/tile-bloom-site/play/) holds Chapter I and the daily tile and needs a connection to load. The chapters after the first, and offline play, are in the iPhone, iPad and Android app.
